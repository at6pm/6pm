# Vietnam's 2027 public holidays, in money: six breaks besides Tết, and what to set aside each month

Between now and the end of 2027, Vietnam has six public holiday breaks besides Tết: 24 November 2026, 1 January, Hùng Kings' Day on 16 April, 30 April–1 May, 2 September and 24 November 2027. For Tết 2027, civil servants are off for seven days, Thursday 4 to Wednesday 10 February. With the example budgets below, the five breaks of 2027 outside Tết cost 9,900,000₫, or 825,000₫ a month on average. But 30 April comes early: start from the 10 November payday and the first six months need 1,100,000₫ each.

## Vietnam's 2027 holiday calendar (plus 24 November this year)

| Holiday                                       | Date                        | Days off                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vietnamese Culture Day                        | Tue 24 Nov 2026             | **Official.** 1 day, the first time                                                                                   |
| New Year's Day                                | Fri 1 Jan 2027              | **Set by law.** 1 day; a three-day weekend if your weekend is Saturday and Sunday                                     |
| Tết (Lunar New Year)                          | Thu 4 – Wed 10 Feb 2027     | **Official.** Civil servants 7 days; businesses at least 5 paid days, on one of three options the employer picks      |
| Hùng Kings' Day (10th of the 3rd lunar month) | Fri 16 Apr 2027             | **Set by law.** 1 day; a three-day weekend                                                                            |
| Reunification Day and Labour Day              | Fri 30 Apr – Sat 1 May 2027 | **Set by law.** 2 holidays; with Saturdays off, 1 May's day moves to Mon 3 May, so **expected** 4 days, 30 Apr – 3 May |
| National Day                                  | Thu 2 – Sun 5 Sep 2027      | **Official.** Civil servants 4 days; businesses give 2 Sep plus 1 or 3 Sep, at the employer's choice                  |
| Vietnamese Culture Day                        | Wed 24 Nov 2027             | **Set by law.** 1 day                                                                                                 |

**Official** means there is a document. In [letter 10065/VPCP-KGVX of 2 October 2026](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/9/10065_vpcp-kgvx_02102026_1-signed.signed.pdf), the Government Office approved seven days off for Tết and four for National Day in 2027, and one day for Vietnamese Culture Day in 2026. The dates are in the Ministry of Home Affairs' notice 11483/TB-BNV of 9 October 2026, as [quoted by Hà Tĩnh's provincial portal](https://hatinh.gov.vn/vi/bai-viet/tet-nguyen-dan-2027-cong-chuc-vien-chuc-duoc-nghi-7-ngay-lien-tuc).

**Set by law** means the day off is already in Article 112 of the 2019 Labour Code (1 January, Hùng Kings' Day, 30 April, 1 May) or in Resolution 28/2026/QH16 (24 November every year). A holiday that lands on your weekly day off moves to the next working day under Article 111. No document yet swaps working days or adds days around these dates, so every longer break here is expected, not official. This page will be updated when a notice comes out.

The calendar above is the civil service one. Private employers choose their Tết days and the extra National Day, and announce them ahead. Shift and service workers may work on the holiday itself. Ask HR for your schedule before you book.

## 825,000₫ is the average. 30 April sets the pace

The holidays don't wait for an even split. If you're paid on the 10th and start with the 10 November 2026 paycheck, there are only six paydays before 30 April (10 Nov, 10 Dec, 10 Jan, 10 Feb, 10 Mar, 10 Apr), and 1 January, Hùng Kings' Day and 30 April already need 6,600,000₫ between them. At 825,000₫ a month you'd have 4,950,000₫ by 30 April, 1,650,000₫ short.

Split by stretch, it works out on time:

| Paydays                             | For                   | Needed         | Each month     |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| 10 Nov 2026 – 10 Apr 2027 (6)       | 1 Jan, 16 Apr, 30 Apr | 6,600,000₫     | **1,100,000₫** |
| 10 May – 10 Aug 2027 (4)            | 2 Sep                 | 3,000,000₫     | **750,000₫**   |
| 10 Sep – 10 Nov 2027 (3)            | 24 Nov                | 300,000₫       | **100,000₫**   |
| **Total: 13 paydays**               |                       | **9,900,000₫** |                |

Check each stop: by 1 January you've put away 2,200,000₫; the beach takes 1,500,000₫, leaving 700,000₫. By Hùng Kings' Day all six paydays are in, so 5,100,000₫ is there; the trip home takes 1,500,000₫, leaving exactly 3,600,000₫ for 30 April.

The first six months are the heavy stretch, and they overlap with saving for Tết. If 1,100,000₫ a month is too tight, each fix has a number:

- **Shrink 30 April**: a 2,400,000₫ trip instead of 3,600,000₫ makes the first stretch (1.5 + 1.5 + 2.4 million) ÷ 6 = 900,000₫ a month.
- **Pay for 1 January from December's money**, like 24 November: the first stretch drops to (1.5 + 3.6 million) ÷ 6 = 850,000₫ a month, and December gets tighter instead.
- **Take fewer trips**: drop one break and share its money among the rest.

Not paid on the 10th? Count your own paydays before each break; the division is the same.

## Start a pot this payday

In 6pm, money set aside for something is a **pot**, with a goal and the month you need it. A pot takes its share before your daily number is worked out: **(goal − what you already have) ÷ months left until that month**, rounded up to the nearest 50,000₫. 6pm wants the money ready before the trip's month begins, so it plans a little tighter than the table above. Made before the 10 November payday:

- A "Beach, 1 Jan" pot, 1,500,000₫, January 2027: 1,500,000 ÷ 2 = **750,000₫** a month.
- A "30 April" pot, 3,600,000₫, April 2027: 3,600,000 ÷ 5 = 720,000₫, rounded up to **750,000₫** a month.

On a 30-day pay cycle, the 30 April pot takes 25,000₫ a day off what's safe to spend today. Seeing that 25,000₫ now is easier than putting the trip on a card in April and paying it off in May.

## FAQ

### How many days off is Tết 2027 in Vietnam?

Civil servants are off seven days, Thursday 4 to Wednesday 10 February 2027 (the first day of Tết is Saturday 6 February). Private-sector workers get at least five paid Tết days; the employer picks one of three options and announces it ahead.

### How many days off are 30 April and 1 May 2027?

By law, two holidays: Friday 30 April and Saturday 1 May. If Saturday is your day off, 1 May's day moves to Monday 3 May, so the expected break is four days, 30 April to 3 May. No notice covers this break yet.

### How many days off is National Day 2027?

Civil servants are off four days, Thursday 2 to Sunday 5 September 2027. Businesses give two: 2 September plus 1 or 3 September, at the employer's choice.

### Is 24 November a public holiday every year?

Yes. Resolution 28/2026/QH16 makes 24 November Vietnamese Culture Day every year, a paid day off. The first is Tuesday 24 November 2026; in 2027 it falls on a Wednesday.
