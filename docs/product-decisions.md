# 6pm v2: product decisions

Decided 10 Oct 2026 unless marked **open**. Change one only with the founder's say-so, and record the change here.
Source of truth for screens, copy and motion is the prototype in `design/prototype/` (inventory: `plans/reports/design-inventory-261010-1732-6pm-v2-prototype-report.md`). Where the prototype contradicts itself or fakes behaviour, this file decides. Defaults below marked *(default)* were chosen by engineering and await founder review.

## 1. What 6pm is

A daily money ritual. Spending is caught all day (shared bank receipts, VietQR scans, forwarded emails, Apple Pay taps, voice, keypad). At the close time (default 18:00) the user swipes through the day in about a minute and wakes up to **one number: safe to spend today**. Pay future you first; everything else is free money split across the days to payday.

Rules that never bend:
- The close, capture and privacy are free forever. Money is never moved by 6pm; it only counts.
- One number. Every screen serves the daily number or the close.
- The companion never shames. Serious mode removes jokes, colour and the companion.
- Native feel first: every transition, gesture and colour matches the prototype; nothing janky ships.

## 3. Money rules (the domain package implements these, with tests)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| M1 | **Unconfirmed spends count.** Caught and pending items reduce today's number until skipped (the number stays honest). The capture-guide line "Nothing counts until you say so" becomes "Nothing is final until you say so" *(default)* |
| M2 | **Days divisor.** `daysLeft` = calendar days from tomorrow through payday (prototype: 9 Oct → 31 Oct = 22). Today's line = startFree ÷ (daysLeft + 1); tomorrow = free ÷ daysLeft. Onboarding uses the same engine, not ÷30/÷31 |
| M2a | **Joining mid-cycle.** In the pay cycle the user set 6pm up in, onboarding asks "Since payday on {date}, about how much have you spent? Not counting bills.", prefilled with the estimate (free money × days before the set-up day ÷ days in the cycle, as if it went at the average rate). That cycle's free money is income − bills − the pots each payday − the answer; skipping keeps the estimate. On Plan the joined cycle's split has a fourth part, "Before" (hatched accent, between Pots and Free; its days have no day dots), and Today's maths reads Free this month · Before you joined · Since you joined · Left · ÷ days; tapping the Before part or its maths row changes the answer while the cycle lasts, and from the next cycle on the part is gone (changed 2026-11-04 from the river's "before you joined" segment). Every later cycle is whole. Plan: `plans/261015-2005-joined-cycle-and-repayments/`. Changed 2026-10-15 from the estimate alone |
| M2b | **Repayments are bills.** Card, trả góp, loan and money owed to a person are bills (the bill deck asks "Paying anything off?"); a bill can have a last month, after which it stops. Card spending counts when it happens. A capture that looks like an unpaid bill's payment is marked as paying it the moment it is caught (a bank alert, a shared receipt, an email, Wallet, Scan, and at the next import or launch for one already waiting; never a spend already answered): the bill shows paid this cycle and the capture never counts in the daily number, so nothing is counted twice. It looks like one when the payee, note or memo names the bill or the same kind of bill (an EVN or SAWACO payment for "Điện nước", VNPT or Viettel for "Điện thoại & mạng"; never power for a phone bill), any day of the bill's cycle while it is unpaid, since utility bills are paid whenever they arrive; or, within a week of its due day, has the bill's exact amount or a repayment word for a bill that is one. Each bill is matched once a cycle, the best match winning. The close still asks once: "Is this your {bill}?", Yes first. Yes keeps it so; No returns it to spending and the bill to due, and the bill is not matched again that cycle. A different amount becomes this cycle's bill, except a card bill, whose payment never moves free money (card spending already counted); confirming one (Yes in the close, or the bill picked in Details) asks once, in the same place, "{bill} is usually {old}. Make it {new} from now on?": "Just this month" (first) or "From now on" (the bill's amount for later cycles). Skipping or deleting the capture puts the bill back to due, undo pays it again, and editing its amount changes this cycle's bill. A repayment word with no matching bill asks "Paying off a card or loan?". No debt balances, interest or payoff maths. Decided 2026-10-15; marked when caught, matched by kind and the amount question changed 2026-11-04 (founder), from asked in the close only, by the same amount or the bill's own name within a week of its due day |
| M3 | **Payday.** Same day each month (clamped to month length), last day of month, or "it varies" (user confirms each payday; until then the cycle is calendar month). Set on one horizontal strip of days, the 1st to the 31st, then "Last day" and "It varies", in onboarding and the month sheet alike; the line under it says what was chosen. Changed 2026-10-30 from a Same day / Last day / It varies segment over a day wheel, founder |
| M4 | **Jars (pots).** Everything saved is a jar. Future you's jar is first on every phone, pinned and never deleted, with no date and an optional target; it fills each payday from the slice the person chose ("pay future you first"), and its name is always said in the phone's language. Every other jar fills **by a date** (`neededBy`: each payday it takes ceil((goal − have) ÷ monthsLeft ÷ 50k) × 50k), **each payday** (a steady `monthly`, up to its target when it has one), or only when added to. The plan's one line, "{x} into pots each payday", is the slice plus every other jar's set-aside; the daily number is income − bills − that line (OCC = the other jars' set-asides). A jar can start with money "Already saved" before 6pm: it counts in `have` and never in this cycle's top-ups. **Updating from 2.1 moves no number:** Future you's jar is made once at start-up (never back-filled: it holds this cycle's part of the slice only, as 2.1 showed no Future you money of its own), and each 2.1 goal keeps filling from the slice by its share, Future you's jar taking what is left, until the person chooses how it fills (by a date or each payday); a rename, an icon, a colour or a target never converts it. Money in saved to Future you goes into its jar, or by the 2.1 goals' shares while any is left. Free: Future you's jar and 3 jars of one's own (dated, each payday, by hand and 2.1 goals all count; Future you's jar and shared Budget together jars never do); someone at or over 3 keeps every jar and adds another only with Max; Max has no limit (founder, 2026-11-04). The Tết preset, Tết words and the Tết question are only for those who keep Tết (a home in đồng or a phone set to Vietnam); everyone else's presets start with a trip. A jar shelf sits on Today under the tonight card, and Plan is one split of the pay into Bills, Jars and Free. Money is added to a jar only from its page (Add); a jar tapped on the shelf, in Plan or in the Pots list opens its page, with no hold to top up, and a top-up reads "Added" in its moves (founder, 2026-11-05). **Add asks where the money comes from** ("This month's money" | "Already saved", remembered per jar on the phone, "This month's money" first): this month's money is a top-up out of free money, the line under showing today's number before → after; already saved is money that was in the bank before, added to what the jar held before 6pm (it counts in `have`, never in this cycle's top-ups, never in the daily number, and this cycle's set-aside stays as it was), the line under saying today's number stays; a dated or each-payday jar shows "{old} → {new} each payday" when the add lowers what it takes from the next payday on. Each add of money already saved is its own line, "Already saved" with its date (founder, 2026-11-05). **Removing a move** (swipe left on a line of a jar's moves, Undo in the pill that follows, which restores exactly): a top-up removed gives its money back to this cycle's free money (one from an earlier cycle stays where it was counted and is taken back out of the jar this cycle); "Jar started" removed sets what it held before 6pm to 0, and the jar's set-aside works itself out again as an edit to it would; an "Already saved" line removed takes only its own amount off what the jar held before 6pm, moving neither today's number nor this cycle's set-aside; a payday's set-aside cannot be removed, since it is what the plan does each payday (founder, 2026-11-05). Changed 2026-11-04 from separate Future you goals and Occasions (founder) |
| M5 | **What-if slider** lives on Future you's jar (and onboarding's first jar), commits Future you on release and the note shows the real change in the daily number (prototype note bug fixed). It runs from 0 to 80% of the plan's income, floored to a notch, and never ends below the amount already set (in onboarding, or on the jar when it was opened), so sliding down never takes it out of reach; with no income it runs to 4tr. Notches are 250k until that makes more than 48, then 500k, 1tr, 2,5tr and up; other currencies scale by value. Past the plan the note says how far past the cycle would be instead of "0 a day", and in a cycle where Future you starts at payday it says no day changes until then. Changed 2026-10-30 from a fixed 0 to 4tr, founder; moved from Plan to Future you's jar 2026-11-04 |
| M6 | **Late items** (caught after the close) reduce the next day's start. **The money day ends at 04:00 everywhere**: before 04:00, Today, the close, the Diary and every capture still belong to the previous day. Decided 2026-10-11 |
| M1a | **Earlier waiting items** (caught or pending from a day already past) join tonight's close and Today's Inbox count, so nothing that counts is ever out of reach. Such an item already counted in the pay cycle it was caught in (M1), so it is listed but never lowers the new cycle's number a second time. Decided 2026-10-11 |
| M7 | **Missed day.** Closing a missed day confirms its items; they were already counted under M1 |
| M8 | **Merchant rules** apply to every new capture (merchant → category). Categories have no limits; `daily: false` excludes a category from the daily number |
| M8a | **Starter categories.** A new install starts with ten: Food, Drinks, Transport, Groceries, Shopping, Fun, Health, Home, Family, Gifts (vi: Ăn uống, Đồ uống, Đi lại, Đi chợ, Mua sắm, Giải trí, Sức khỏe, Nhà cửa, Gia đình, Quà tặng). Bills and rent stay in the plan's Bills, not a category, so they are not counted twice. They are seeded only when an install has no categories, so an install that already has its own keeps them as they are and gains none. Founder request 2026-10-14 |
| M8b | **Category icon follows its name.** While a category is named in the editor, its icon is chosen for it: the phone's own word match first (English and Vietnamese, accents optional, works offline), then, after a pause in the typing and only when the phone found nothing, `POST /v1/category-icon`, where Claude picks one icon from the icons the person may use. Only the name and the icon list are sent. An icon the person picks by hand is never replaced, and renaming an existing category changes its icon only while it still wears the icon its old name pointed to. Founder request 2026-10-14 |
| M8c | **Deleting and ordering categories (founder, 2026-11-05).** Swipe a category left to delete it (as a Today row); full swipe deletes, with Undo in the undo bar that puts it back exactly. Its spends move so none changes whether it counts in the day: a daily category's to "Other" ("Khác"), a not-daily one's to "Other · not daily", each made the first time it is needed ("Moved 12 to Khác"); deleting Other or Other · not daily itself moves its spends to the first other category of the same kind, else the first in the person's order. Its rules go. The last category can never be deleted. Hold a category to lift it and drag it to a new place; the order is saved at once and every list of categories follows it. "New category" stays last. With Max, the icon picker shows no free-or-Max counts or locks. |
| M9 | **Duplicates.** Same amount, different capture sources (QR, shared receipt, email, Apple Pay, bank alert), within 3 minutes → merged into one item with a source trail ("Seen 2 ways"). A QR scan still waiting to be paid also merges with a same-amount bank capture (email, shared receipt, Apple Pay, alert) arriving from its tap up to 20 minutes after it, never before. A bank's email or alert, written after the payment, merges with another sighting up to 15 minutes after it (still 3 minutes before it); every other pair keeps the 3 minutes. Two emails from different senders (the bank's alert and the shop's receipt for one order), or two alerts from different apps (a wallet and the bank it drew from), are two sources; two from one sender or app never merge. A payment already seen by a source never takes a second sighting from it (that is a second payment), except one bank notification caught twice (the automation running again, or the bank posting the same words again): an alert with the same app and the same words (title, subtitle and body) within 3 minutes of one already saved is the same notification and is dropped, never a second spend or sighting. The words are never kept: an alert's id starts with a fingerprint of them. Two payments of the same amount from one bank differ in their words (the balance after each), so they stay two; alerts caught before ids carried the fingerprint are known by bank, amount and the same delivery instant. Two notices (emails or alerts) of one charge abroad from different senders, with the same amount to the cent in the currency paid and both naming the same payee, merge up to 6 hours apart (a card's email and the shop's own receipt). The merged item, whichever sighting came first, is named by the sighting that says most about who was paid: a named payee beats a blank or a bank's or transfer's label ("Techcombank QR", "MBCT"), and any other source's name (an email's payee, a receipt, Wallet, a QR) beats a bank alert's, which is often only the transfer's memo; the category comes with the name, unless the user already kept the spend. Between two names of one payee that say as much, the shop's spelling replaces a bank's capitals ("Cloudflare" for "CLOUDFLARE"). Money in merges only with money in, named the same way (a refund email's shop over the bank alert's memo). With no same-amount match, two sightings at most 1% apart (a fee: 32.000₫ and 32.300₫) merge when their payees do not name two different shops. A charge abroad is matched by the amount in the currency paid, before either sighting is priced (both decided 2026-10-16). Money in and money out never merge, so a refund never merges with the spend it gives back. Something the user typed (Quick add, voice, cash) is never merged with anything. Cancelling a scanned payment takes back only the scan's own sighting. 20-minute QR window decided 2026-10-16; the 15-minute notice window, the alert sources, the 1% fee and the trail rule follow the founder's week of forwarded mail, 2026-10-27; repeated alerts, the 6-hour window abroad and the richest name follow the founder's doubled Inbox, 2026-10-30 |
| M10 | **Work spends.** The close asks once a day, about the largest waiting spend of 100.000₫ or more read from a bank receipt or email *(default)*. Marked "for work" in the close → Claim back, excluded from the daily number |
| M11 | Rounding: daily lines floor to one **step**; pot set-asides ceil to 50 steps. A step is what 1.000₫ is to the design: 1.000₫, $1, ¥10, ₩100, Rp1.000 (from the currency's rough value, never below one unit). Amounts the design names in đồng scale by value (thresholds, chart scales, starting goals, presets) or by step (cash chips, steppers, the keypad's k). Formatting: VND exactly per design (dot thousands, `₫`, U+2212 minus, `tr`/`k`/`tỷ` short forms); other currencies with the phone's separators and symbol position, cents only when an amount has them, short forms `$353`, `$17.4k`, `$1.2M`. Decided 2026-10-15 |
| M12 | **Money in.** Money that came in (pay, a gift, a side job, a refund, someone paying back) is caught like spending (bank alerts, receipt email read as incoming or a refund, voice: "mẹ cho 500k") and is never spending. It changes nothing until the user places it at the close: **spread** (right swipe; added to this cycle's free money, so every day left gets a share), **Future you** (up swipe; a top-up of Future you's jar, or of the 2.1 goals by their shares while any is left, so today's number holds), or from Edit a **jar** or **paying me back**. The close asks first when it looks like something: **the pay** (at least 70% of the plan's income, within 3 days of payday; yes keeps the plan's income as the pay and peels anything above it off as its own card, with "Make it {pay}" once the extra is 5% of pay or more), **a refund** (a refund word or the same shop, within 60 days, for at most the spend; yes strikes the spend through in the Diary and gives its money back to the days), **a payback** (the exact amount of a work claim within 120 days, or of a spend with a note, a split, within 21 days; yes settles it and the number does not move, since only the user's share counted). No user default: the user decides each time; money of 20% of the income or more gets the companion's "big one" line instead of the hint. The plan's income is **what you can count on**, the least that lands each month; anything above comes in through the close. Money in is in no spending total (Ask, the Diary, insights, the river's spent), is written below zero in the CSV, and the river's total is income plus money in kept. No Quick add tab for it: voice and capture only. Free. Founder 2026-10-27 |
| M13 | **Spending from a jar.** A spend marked "From {jar}" (`fromPot`, with the part the jar covered) is spending everywhere it is history (Diary, week recap, insights, Ask, CSV, year book, the receipt's lines), shown as "From {jar}"; only the jar's part stays out of today's number and free money. A jar never goes below zero: it covers what it holds and the rest counts today. Skipping, deleting, editing or undoing the spend moves the jar with it. A spend removed from a jar's moves is no longer from the jar: it counts as spending that day again, as turning "From a jar" off does, and its undo puts back the same jar and the same part it covered (founder, 2026-11-05). A receipt and the Diary print each such spend in full and, under the spends, one "From {jar}" line per jar that takes its part off, so the lines add up to the day's total (what the number counted). **Deleting a jar:** its spends keep their cover (never more than it held); its set-aside and top-ups this cycle go back to free money less what was already spent from it this cycle, so no money counts free twice. The close asks "From your {jar}?" only about a jar the person armed: one they took a spend from by hand (Details' From a jar, Quick add, or the jar's Spend from it) that still stands from it; the close's own yes never arms one, and its "No, count it today" disarms that jar until the next spend taken from it by hand (remembered on the phone, never in the backup; undoing or deleting the hand spend disarms it too; founder, 2026-11-04). Even then only for a dated jar that is due (in its own month, or a trip jar while Travel mode is on, up to its month), once a night, about the largest such spend (as the work question does); never Future you or a jar with no date (taken from by choice in Details or Quick add), and never because the spend names the jar. The Tết window is not asked about yet (the app has no lunar table); a Tết jar is asked in its month like any dated jar. A jar is **ready** when full or when its month comes; the close shows it once per jar and reason, remembered on the phone. Quick add asks with one "From a jar" switch in its header (a jar glyph): off, the spend counts today; on, one row of the jars holding something opens in the sheet, each with what it holds, and the first that holds it all is picked; the sheet is as tall as what it holds and grows and shrinks with the row (founder, 2026-11-05). Added 2026-11-04 (founder) |
| M14 | **History brought in from another app.** Money Lover's, MISA's and MoMo's exports (and any CSV or Excel file whose columns the person names) are read on the phone and brought in as kept spending and money in with source `import` and the app it came from. They are history only: the Diary (outlined days, "Brought in"), Ask and insights where their windows reach, the CSV export; they never change today's number, a close, a bill, a jar or money in, and are never asked about in a close. Only lines dated before the day the person started 6pm (the plan's start day) come in; lines from days 6pm covers are left out and counted as "already in 6pm". Lines inside the joined pay cycle (before the start day) replace onboarding's "spent since payday" estimate with their real daily spending while that cycle lasts (M2a); undoing puts the answer back. Set aside and counted apart: failed payments, moves between the person's own accounts (MoMo's Túi Thần Tài in and out, Money Lover's transfers, MISA's chuyển khoản), balance fixes, loans and debts, and lines in another currency; money in stays money in, never spending. Imports never train merchant rules and suggest no income or payday. The same file brought in twice adds nothing (each line's id is the app, its date, amount and words, and which of its kind it is). Undo is a hold of 1.2 s on the bring-in's row at the foot of the Bring your history guide (moved from Settings › Your data, founder 2026-11-05), no confirm sheet, and removes only that bring-in's lines. MoMo's locked file is opened with the birthday the person types, never kept. Only an unknown file's headers and value shapes, and category names 6pm's own words could not place (never a MoMo history's shops or people, an amount, a date or a note), may go to `POST /v1/import/map`. Free for everyone. Founder, 2026-11-05. **Labels (founder, 2026-11-05):** a bring-in never makes a category per label. Every label goes in one of the person's own categories, a starter (or Bills) they lack, made once, or Not daily, which is one shared "Other · not daily" ("Khác · không tính ngày"), found by its id or its name in any of the ten languages and made once; undoing the bring-in that filled it puts it away when nothing else is in it. 6pm's words guess on whole words and phrases only, and Not daily only from a whole category name ("Phí", "Đầu tư"), never from a word inside a shop's or a person's name. A MoMo history's labels are payees: a shop's or a bill's words (Quick add's and the bill hints) place it; a person (a full name in capitals), a transfer and anything unplaced are left for the person to pick. Guesses show as suggested (a dashed chip, "Suggested") until the person picks; Bring in fills only when every label has a home. Paying off MoMo's pay-later balance is set aside as a debt: its spending already came in line by line. On launch, categories an earlier bring-in made per label, untouched since (name, colour, icon, not daily) and holding only brought-in lines, are archived and their lines move to "Other · not daily" (or to the person's own not-daily Bills for a bill's payee), so no day, total or closed day moves; a category the person renamed, changed or used stays. |
